Programme Guidelines

A few things to know before you refer.

Siyasizana is available to qualifying existing ThuthukaSA customers.

01

Existing customers

You must be an existing ThuthukaSA customer to participate in the referral programme.

02

Policy in good standing

Your qualifying policy must be in force and premiums must be up to date.

03

Consent matters

Obtain explicit consent before sharing your referral's contact information.

04

Three successful premiums

The referral reward qualifies after three successful premiums have been deducted.
